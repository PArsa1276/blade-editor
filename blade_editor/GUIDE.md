# Blade Editor: short guide

Blade Editor is installed. You only say what you want; Claude does the editing.

## 1. Start

1. Put your clips, one music file and (optional) a video whose style you like in one folder.
2. Open that folder in Claude (Claude app: **Code** -> choose the folder; terminal: `claude` in the folder).
3. Write your request in your own language.

## 2. Say it like this

- "15-second vertical edit of these clips on song.mp3, hype style, title «My Trip». Preview first."
- "Copy the editing style of ref.mp4 (cuts, camera moves, effects) onto my clips."
- "Remove the pauses from talk.mp4 and add word-by-word captions."
- "Clip 2: slow motion at second 3, stabilize it, then a 360 spin that stops at each side."
- "Animated intro title with Kinetograph." / "Make a missing shot with Google Flow."

Good to add: length, format (9:16, 1:1, 16:9), and what must not change.

## 3. Change and finish

Say what to change: "faster cuts", "fewer flashes", "yellow title", "logo top right".
When you like it: "final version and quality check". The file is in your folder.

## 4. Styles

**hype** (cut on every beat, punches, flashes) · **phonk** (black-and-white, much shake) ·
**cinematic** (slow, film look) · **smooth** (calm, warm) · **glitch** (digital glitches) ·
**velocity** (fast, then slow motion with a flash on the strong beat).

## 5. Google Flow (optional)

One time, add the **Blade Bridge** extension to your normal Chrome: open `chrome://extensions`,
turn on **Developer mode**, click **Load unpacked** and choose the folder `.blade-editor/chrome-extension`
in your home folder. After that just ask for a clip: Blade opens Google Flow itself, with your
own Google login. You see the prompt first; credits are spent only after you say yes. Automating Flow may be against Google's
terms - your choice.

## 6. If something is wrong

Tell Claude what you see, for example "the text looks wrong", or paste the error. Claude does
not repeat the same analysis many times: if a measurement is unsure, it tells you once.
